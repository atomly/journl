# Approved brand artwork

Use assets/brand/lockup-light.svg on light surfaces and lockup-dark.svg on dark surfaces. These are the approved Shifted right Gather assets. Use mark-*.svg for symbol-only placements and icon-*-{16,24,32,48}.png for raster icons. Wordmarks are outlined SVG paths and do not depend on font installation.

The upper-left block is at x8,y0 with width24,height20 in the 64×64 viewBox. The right block starts at x36,y0 and ends at y38. The lower-right gap remains 4 units. Do not redraw, round, rotate or recolor the artwork.

The renderer preserves the existing brand slot and uniformly fits the lockup inside it. Application navigation and authentication use a maximum 40px lockup height. Marketing branding uses 32px height; the artwork can extend into existing free space beside its original brand slot without moving neighboring controls. Hidden/collapsed navigation does not add a new logo or alter control placement. Mobile navigation sheets use the same artwork. In forced-colors scenes, use the monochrome ink artwork on the system canvas.

Ask Journl remains the assistant name; it is not replaced with a second brand logo. All current approved screenshots are under views/. Historical logo variants are not included.
